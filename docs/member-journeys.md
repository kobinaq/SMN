# Member Journeys

**Updated:** 2026-07-21

## Join and orient

1. Create an account at /signup.
2. Sign in at /login and land on /app.
3. Use the non-blocking onboarding checklist to complete profile, skills, interests, and goals.
4. Follow the primary continuity action into the next available activity.

## Learn

1. Open /app/learning or /app/learning/courses.
2. Start or resume the last in-progress lesson.
3. Use module/lesson navigation and approved video, reading, link, and file resources.
4. Mark progress; the interface confirms success or offers a retry.
5. Complete the course and follow certificate eligibility when enabled.

Access may come from a direct enrollment, programme key, paid checkout, cohort, workshop, or staff grant. Classroom links appear only after the relevant access is granted.

## Mentorship

1. Browse /app/mentors.
2. Review availability and submit a focused request.
3. Track pending or active mentorship state from the member experience.
4. Staff—not AI—review applications and coordinate introductions.

## Opportunities

1. Browse /app/opportunities and filter current listings.
2. Open the detail page and continue to the employer’s application site.
3. Record personal tracking state in SMN. SMN does not auto-apply or make hiring decisions.
4. Use Career Coach only when enabled; review evidence/gaps and confirm before saving plans.

## Portfolio and certificates

1. Create or edit a portfolio case study at /app/portfolio.
2. Keep it draft/private until the public preview is ready.
3. View credentials at /app/certificates.
4. Share only public, valid verification links.

## Events and tickets

1. Browse the public events page and open an event.
2. Sign in when member identity is required.
3. For a free event, confirm registration directly.
4. For a paid event, complete Paystack checkout and return to verification.
5. Find registrations and tickets under /app/events and /app/events/tickets.
6. Use the access link only when the event/registration makes it available.
7. Contact operations for cancellation or refund questions; a cancellation does not necessarily mean a Paystack refund has occurred.

## Privacy and support

Member and staff sessions are separate. Members control their profile, portfolio visibility, and retained Career Coach state where available. Report incorrect access, payment, ticket, certificate, or AI behavior without sending passwords, card information, or private prompt content.
